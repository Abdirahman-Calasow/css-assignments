Arthur Pendelton

Master Clockmaker & Time-Traveler

Arthur Pendelton spent forty years repairing antique pocket watches in a quiet corner of London before discovering a hidden gear mechanism that allowed him to step through temporal cracks. Now, he spends his weekends visiting historical eras, documenting forgotten stories, and accidentally leaving behind modern umbrellas.
